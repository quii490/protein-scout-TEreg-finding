# DCXR - Rejection Report

## Rejection Summary

**Gene:** DCXR
**Protein:** L-xylulose reductase
**UniProt:** Q7Z4W1

**Reason for rejection:** DCXR is an L-xylulose reductase that catalyzes NADPH-dependent reduction of pentoses, tetroses, and alpha-dicarbonyl compounds. It participates in the uronate cycle of glucose metabolism and plays a role in water absorption and cellular osmoregulation in proximal renal tubules. While DCXR has a nuclear GO-CC annotation (one of ten cellular compartment annotations), its primary localization is at the apical plasma membrane, cytoplasmic microtubules, microvilli, and acrosomal vesicles. UniProt lists the primary subcellular location as "Apical cell membrane" and "Cytoplasmic vesicle, secretory vesicle, acrosome." The nuclear annotation is an outlier among membrane and cytoskeletal compartment annotations. DCXR has no evidence of nuclear function and no connection to TE regulation. The primary function in carbohydrate metabolism and osmoregulation at the apical membrane makes nuclear localization functionally implausible as a primary activity site.
