# ATP23 - Rejection Report

## Rejection Summary

**Gene:** ATP23
**Protein:** Mitochondrial inner membrane protease ATP23 homolog
**UniProt:** Q9Y6H3

**Reason for rejection:** ATP23 is a mitochondrial inner membrane protease. Its GO-CC annotations include cell junction, cytosol, DNA-dependent protein kinase-DNA ligase 4 complex, mitochondrion, and plasma membrane. Despite the DNA-PK complex annotation (which could suggest a DNA repair connection), the protein name and primary annotation clearly identify it as a mitochondrial protein. UniProt lists no SUBCELLULAR LOCATION annotation, and no functional characterization text is available. There is no nuclear GO-CC term. The annotation to DNA-PK complex is suspicious and may represent a high-throughput interaction artifact rather than functional localization. No evidence supports nuclear function or TE regulatory relevance.
