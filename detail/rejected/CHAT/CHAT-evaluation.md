# CHAT - Rejection Report

## Rejection Summary

**Gene:** CHAT
**Protein:** Choline O-acetyltransferase
**UniProt:** P28329

**Reason for rejection:** CHAT catalyzes the reversible synthesis of acetylcholine from acetyl CoA and choline at cholinergic synapses. While CHAT has a nuclear GO-CC annotation (one of five: cytoplasm, cytosol, neuron projection, nucleus, synapse), its primary functional localization is cytoplasmic and synaptic. UniProt provides no SUBCELLULAR LOCATION annotation, and there is no evidence that CHAT has a nuclear function. The nuclear GO-CC annotation likely reflects incidental detection, possibly from perinuclear aggregation or detection during nuclear envelope breakdown. Acetylcholine synthesis is a cytoplasmic/synaptic process. CHAT has no known role in chromatin biology, RNA processing, or TE regulation.
