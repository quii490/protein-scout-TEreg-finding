# ACR - Rejection Report

## Rejection Summary

**Gene:** ACR
**Protein:** Acrosin
**UniProt:** P10323

**Reason for rejection:** ACR (acrosin) is a sperm-specific serine protease stored in the acrosome, a specialized organelle in sperm cells. Its GO-CC annotations include acrosomal matrix, extracellular region, Golgi-associated vesicle, nucleus, and protein-containing complex. The nuclear GO-CC annotation is either incidental or reflects nuclear localization during a transient stage of spermatogenesis (nuclear condensation). ACR's sole function is in fertilization - it is synthesized as proacrosin in the acrosome and functions in penetrating the zona pellucida of the oocyte. It is a highly specialized reproductive protein with no relevance to somatic TE regulation. The sperm-specific expression pattern and acrosomal function make this protein irrelevant to nuclear TE biology in physiologically meaningful contexts.
