# ACPP (ACP3) - Rejection Report

## Rejection Summary

**Gene:** ACPP (ACP3)
**Protein:** Prostatic acid phosphatase
**UniProt:** P15309

**Reason for rejection:** ACPP is a secreted prostatic acid phosphatase. Despite having a nuclear GO-CC annotation present in the annotation set, ACPP is primarily a secreted protein found in semen (where it forms SEVI amyloid fibrils that enhance HIV infection). Its functional localization is extracellular, at the plasma membrane, and in lysosomes. The nuclear GO-CC annotation likely reflects incidental detection in high-throughput studies rather than functional nuclear localization. There is no evidence that ACPP has a nuclear function relevant to TE regulation. The secreted nature and well-established extracellular/lysosomal function make this protein irrelevant to nuclear TE biology.
