### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

K(+) channel that conducts outward rectifying tonic currents potentiated by purinergic signals (PubMed:24163367, PubMed:25148687, PubMed:30472253, PubMed:38409076). Homo- and heterodimerizes to form functional channels with distinct regulatory and gating properties (PubMed:25148687, PubMed:40011745, PubMed:40011746, PubMed:40307591, PubMed:40178898). Contributes most of K(+) currents at the plasma
