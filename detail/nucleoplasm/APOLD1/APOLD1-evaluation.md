### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Is a modulator of endothelial barrier permeability, required for proper organization of endothelial cell-cell junctions and cytoskeleton (PubMed:35638551). It also plays a role in the modulation of secretory autophagy (PubMed:35638551). May affect blood-brain barrier permeability
