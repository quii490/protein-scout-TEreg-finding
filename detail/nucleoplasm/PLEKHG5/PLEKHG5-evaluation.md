**蛋白全称**: Pleckstrin homology domain-containing family G member 5

**功能**: Functions as a guanine exchange factor (GEF) for RAB26 and thus regulates autophagy of synaptic vesicles in axon terminal of motoneurons (By similarity). Involved in the control of neuronal cell differentiation (PubMed:11704860). Plays a role in angiogenesis through regulation of endothelial cells chemotaxis.
