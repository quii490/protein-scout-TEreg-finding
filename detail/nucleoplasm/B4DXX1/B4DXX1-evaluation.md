### 补充分析 (UniProt API)

**蛋白全称**: tRNA (guanine(26)-N(2))-dimethyltransferase

**功能**: Specifically dimethylates a single guanine residue at position 27 of tRNA(Tyr) using S-adenosyl-L-methionine as donor of the methyl groups. Dimethylation at position 27 of tRNA(Tyr) is required for efficient translation of tyrosine codons. Also required to maintain 3-(3-amino-3-carboxypropyl)uridine (acp3U) in the D-loop of several cytoplasmic tRNAs
