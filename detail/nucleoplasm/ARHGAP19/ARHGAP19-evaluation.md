### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Has GTPase activator activity toward RHOA; RHOA activation results in GTP hydrolysis and conversion from an active GTP-bound to an inactive GDP-bound state (PubMed:24259668, PubMed:41086021). Lacks GTPase activator activity toward RAC1 or CDC42 (PubMed:24259668)
