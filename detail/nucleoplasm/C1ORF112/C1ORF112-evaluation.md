### 补充分析 (UniProt API)

**蛋白全称**: FIGNL1-interacting regulator of recombination and mitosis

**功能**: Forms a complex with FIGL1, which regulates DNA double-strand break (DSB) repair via homologous recombination (HR) (PubMed:29608566, PubMed:37256941, PubMed:37347663, PubMed:37515771, PubMed:37556550, PubMed:38286805). The FIGNL1-FIRRM complex is essential for resolution of homologous recombination intermediates in response to DNA interstrand cross-links (ICLs) (PubMed:37256941, PubMed:37347663, PubMed:37515771, PubMed:37556550, PubMed:38286805). The complex regulates HR by promoting disassembly
