**蛋白全称**: Opsin-3

**功能**: G protein-coupled photoreceptor that selectively activates G(i/o) proteins in response to light, thereby decreasing intracellular cAMP levels (PubMed:28842328, PubMed:30168605, PubMed:31380578, PubMed:36869204). Activation occurs when the opsin-bound cis-retinal chromophore absorbs a photon and isomerizes to all-trans-retinal, inducing a conformational change in the opsin that triggers a G protein-mediated phototransduction cascade (PubMed:36869204).
