### 补充分析 (UniProt API)

**蛋白全称**: Bifunctional 3'-phosphoadenosine 5'-phosphosulfate synthase 2

**功能**: Bifunctional enzyme with both ATP sulfurylase and APS kinase activity, which mediates two steps in the sulfate activation pathway. The first step is the transfer of a sulfate group to ATP to yield adenosine 5'-phosphosulfate (APS), and the second step is the transfer of a phosphate group from ATP to APS yielding 3'-phosphoadenylylsulfate/PAPS, the activated sulfate donor used by sulfotransferases (PubMed:11773860, PubMed:19474428, PubMed:23824674, PubMed:25594860).
