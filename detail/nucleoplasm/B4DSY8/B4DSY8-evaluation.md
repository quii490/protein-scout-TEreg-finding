### 补充分析 (UniProt API)

**蛋白全称**: Structure-specific endonuclease ANKLE1

**功能**: Structure-selective DNA endonuclease that specifically cleaves branched DNA. Processes chromatin bridges during cytokinesis by cleaving DNA, both priming TREX1-mediated resolution and directly resolving bridges. Proper processing of chromatin bridges prevents their breakage during cytokinesis, thereby reducing DNA damage, micronuclei formation, and the activation of immune responses such as the cGAS-STING pathway
