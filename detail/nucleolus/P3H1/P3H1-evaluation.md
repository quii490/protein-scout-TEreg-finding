### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Has prolyl 3-hydroxylase activity and catalyzes the post-translational formation of 3-hydroxyproline in -Xaa-Pro-Gly- sequences in pro-collagen chains, a critical step for the formation of mature collagen trimers (PubMed:39245686). May be involved in the secretory pathway of cells. Has growth suppressive activity in fibroblasts
