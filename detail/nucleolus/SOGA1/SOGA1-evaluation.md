### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Microtubule-associated factor that enables integration of the centrosomal and Golgi-associated microtubules on the Golgi membrane, supporting directional migration. Preferentially acts on the perinuclear microtubules accumulated around the Golgi.
