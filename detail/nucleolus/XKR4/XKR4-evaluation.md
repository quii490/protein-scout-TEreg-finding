### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Phospholipid scramblase that promotes phosphatidylserine exposure on apoptotic cell surface (PubMed:25231987, PubMed:33725486, PubMed:37696806). Phosphatidylserine is a specific marker only present at the surface of apoptotic cells and acts as a specific signal for engulfment (PubMed:25231987, PubMed:37696806)
