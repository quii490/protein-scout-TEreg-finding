### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Microtubule inner protein (MIP) part of the dynein-decorated doublet microtubules (DMTs) in cilia axoneme, which is required for motile cilia beating (PubMed:36191189). Regulates motility patterns of both 9+0 and 9+2 motile cilia through differential localization and recruitment of axonemal dynein components (By similarity). Required for centriolar satellite integrity and non-motile cilium assembl
