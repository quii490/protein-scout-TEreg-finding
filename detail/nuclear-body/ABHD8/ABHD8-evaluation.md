### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Negatively regulates NLRP3-driven inflammation (PubMed:39225180). Promotes NLRP3 degradation through the chaperone-mediated autophagy (CMA) pathway, hence attenuating inflammasome activation and IL1B secretion. Acts by recruiting palmitoyltransferase ZDHHC12 to NLRP3, facilitating NLRP3 palmitoylation and subsequent degradation (PubMed:39225180)
