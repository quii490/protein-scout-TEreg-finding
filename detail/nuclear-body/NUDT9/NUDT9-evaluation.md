### 3. 详细分析

#### 3.1 功能描述

Hydrolyzes ADP-ribose (ADPR) to AMP and ribose 5'-phosphate (PubMed:11385575, PubMed:37343711). Has also a weak phosphohydrolase activity against NADH and FAD (in vitro) (PubMed:37343711)
