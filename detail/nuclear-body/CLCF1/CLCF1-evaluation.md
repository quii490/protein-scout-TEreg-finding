Functions as a cytokine, either alone or by forming a stable complex with soluble CRLF1 or CNTFR (PubMed:10448081, PubMed:10500198, PubMed:10966616, PubMed:11285233, PubMed:11294841). Binds to CNTFR complex (CNTFR, IL6ST/gp130, LIFR) or can signal via trans-signaling using soluble CNTFR subunits (PubMed:10966616, PubMed:11285233, PubMed:11294841).
