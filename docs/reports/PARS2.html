3. 详细分析

3.1 功能描述

Mitochondrial aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase that catalyzes the specific attachment of the proline amino acid (aa) to the homologous transfer RNA (tRNA), further participating in protein synthesis. The reaction occurs in a two steps: proline is first activated by ATP to form Pro-AMP and then transferred to the acceptor end of tRNA(Pro)

深度机制分析

结构域架构：PARS2（475 aa, 52.2 kDa, Q7L3T8, Probable proline--tRNA ligase, mitochondrial）是II类氨酰tRNA合成酶（aaRS）家族成员——催化线粒体Pro-tRNA(Pro)的合成。结构域组成：（1）Class II aaRS core catalytic domain（IPR006195, IPR002314, Pfam PF00587, tRNA-synt_2b）——Class II aaRS的标志性fold为central 7-stranded anti-parallel beta-sheet surrounded by alpha-helices——active site含conserved motifs 1/2/3（motif 1参与dimer interface, motif 2参与amino acid activation, motif 3参与tRNA acceptor stem binding）——催化proline activation（Pro + ATP → Pro-AMP + PPi）和transferred to tRNA(Pro) 3' terminal A76。（2）Anticodon binding domain（IPR045864, Pfam PF03129, HGTP_anticodon）——位于C-terminal——识别tRNA(Pro) anticodon stem-loop（IGG/UGG anticodon in human mt-tRNA(Pro)）。（3）N-terminal mitochondrial targeting signal（MTS）——引导pre-PARS2从胞质ribosome进入线粒体intermembrane space→经TOM/TIM translocase进入线粒体matrix→MTS被mitochondrial processing peptidase（MPP）切除生成mature PARS2。AlphaFold结构可用——class II aaRS fold高度保守，pLDDT>=85在catalytic domain，anticodon binding domain pLDDT~70-80。PDB尚无实验结构。此外，PARS2含新近发现的Zinc-binding domain（PMID:41720228）——variant interpretation study揭示新的structural zinc coordination site——可能在dimerization或tRNA recognition中发挥作用。PARS2的线粒体特异性使其与胞质ProRS（EPRS/EARS2）在底物（mt-tRNA(Pro) vs cyt-tRNA(Pro)）和compartment上区分。

PPI互作网络解读：PPI degree有限，反映PARS2作为线粒体protein synthesis机器组分。EPRS（Glutamyl-prolyl-tRNA synthetase, BioGRID）是胞质bifunctional aaRS——含glutamyl-tRNA synthetase（EARS）和prolyl-tRNA synthetase（PARS）两个catalytic domains——PARS2线粒体ProRS与EPRS胞质ProRS共享底物（proline + ATP）但分别负责不同compartment的Pro-tRNA(Pro)合成。PRKAA2（AMPK alpha-2 catalytic subunit, BioGRID）是cellular energy sensor——AMPK在mitochondrial stress时phosphorylate PGC-1alpha→调节线粒体biogenesis——PRKAA2-PARS2互作暗示PARS2可能集成AMPK-dependent mitochondrial translation调控。ICT1（Immature colon carcinoma transcript 1/MRPL58, BioGRID）是mitochondrial ribosome large subunit（mt-LSU/mitoribosome）的component——作为peptidyl-tRNA hydrolase rescue stalled mitochondrial ribosome——在mitochondrial translation quality control中功能。DLD（Dihydrolipoamide dehydrogenase, BioGRID）是mitochondrial pyruvate dehydrogenase complex（PDC）和alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase complex的E3 subunit——连接mitochondrial energy metabolism和protein synthesis。LMX1B（LIM homeobox transcription factor 1 beta, BioGRID）是development transcription factor——在肾小球podocyte和dopaminergic neuron specification中功能。FOXM1（Forkhead box M1, BioGRID）是cell cycle-dependent transcription factor——调控G2/M transition gene expression。

结构解读：Class II aaRS催化Pro-tRNA(Pro)合成的two-step reaction：（1）Adenylation——Pro + ATP → Pro-AMP + PPi——proline的pyrrolidine ring side chain插入active site hydrophobic pocket——motif 2 Arg/Lys residues form salt bridge with ATP phosphate；（2）tRNA charging——Pro-AMP + tRNA(Pro)-3'OH → Pro-tRNA(Pro) + AMP——tRNA(Pro) acceptor stem的discriminator base（A73）和CCA 3'-terminus经class II aaRS的conserved Arg/Lys/Ser residues recognition——transition state stabilization经Mg2+-dependent mechanism完成。Zinc-binding domain（PMID:41720228）为新发现的structural domain——zinc finger-like motif（CxxC...CxxC/His pattern）在class II aaRS中不常见——可能在PARS2 dimer formation或mt-tRNA(Pro) recognition中发挥独特功能。

机制模型：（1）Mitochondrial translation——PARS2催化形成Pro-tRNA(Pro)——进入mt-ribosome A site——Pro被incorporate至nascent mtDNA-encoded polypeptide chain（MT-ND1-6, MT-CO1-3, MT-CYTB, MT-ATP6/8）——Pro作为secondary structure breaker（pyrrolidine ring rigidity）在mitochondrial OXPHOS subunit的transmembrane helix boundary和turn region中频率较高。（2）Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy (DEE)——PARS2 variants导致mitochondrial translation缺陷——mitochondrial proline-tRNA synthetase deficiency→OXPHOS subunit synthesis减少→mitochondrial respiratory chain dysfunction→oxidative stress→integrated stress response (ISR) activation（PMID:41380592）——ATF4-CHOP-GADD34 axis→global translational reprogramming→neuronal susceptibility to excitotoxicity→epileptic seizure and intellectual disability。（3）Ferroptosis——PARS2 deficiency导致mitochondrial homeostasis impairment and ferroptotic activation（PMID:42259431）——mitochondrial dysfunction增加ROS production→lipid peroxidation→ferroptosis execution in neuronal cells。（4）Mitochondrial cardiomyopathy（PMID:39253392）——compound heterozygous PARS2 variants→lethal mitochondrial cardiomyopathy——demonstrating PARS2在心肌（high mitochondrial energy demand）中的essential role。

TE调控展望：PARS2的TE调控为高度间接途径——经mitochondrial-to-nuclear retrograde signaling。PARS2 deficiency导致mitochondrial translation defect→mitochondrial stress→ISR activation（ATF4-CHOP axis）→ATF4作为transcription factor结合C/EBP-ATF response element(CARE)——该motif在HERV LTR及LINE-1 5'UTR中分布——ISR-dependent ATF4 activation may transcriptionally activate TE expression（已有文献报道ATF4 binding ERV LTR驱动其expression）。FOXM1作为cell cycle transcription factor——FOXM1 binding sequences（FHK consensus TAAACA）在LINE-1 promoter中存在——FOXM1在多种肿瘤中activate LINE-1 expression——PARS2-FOXM1互作可能functionally link mitochondrial translation to cell cycle-dependent TE activation。EPRS（胞质ProRS）作为aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase——经MSC（multi-tRNA synthetase complex）整合翻译信号——MSC在TE mRNA translation elongation phase的tRNA supply中发挥作用——PARS2-EPRS互作可能经mitochondrial-cytosolic tRNA synthetase crosstalk影响TE translation efficiency。但这些TE调控路径均为retrograde signaling的indirect consequence——直接TE调控不是PARS2的primary function。
