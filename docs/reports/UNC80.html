Auxilary subunit of the NALCN channelosome complex, which regulates the resting membrane potential by depolarizing sodium leak currents (PubMed:32494638, PubMed:35387979, PubMed:34929720). The NALCN channelosome complex is a voltage-gated ion channel responsible for the resting Na(+) permeability that controls neuronal excitability (PubMed:35387979, PubMed:34929720).
