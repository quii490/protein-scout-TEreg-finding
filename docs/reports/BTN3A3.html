BTN3A3 - Rejection Report

Rejection Summary

Gene: BTN3A3 Protein: Butyrophilin subfamily 3 member A3 UniProt: O00478

Reason for rejection: BTN3A3 is a plasma membrane protein of the butyrophilin family that plays a role in T-cell responses in the adaptive immune response. Its GO-CC annotations are exclusively membrane-associated: external side of plasma membrane, membrane, plasma membrane. UniProt lists its sole subcellular location as "Cell membrane." There is no nuclear GO-CC term and no evidence of nuclear localization or function. BTN3A3 is an immunoglobulin superfamily member that functions in immune cell surface signaling. There is no connection to nuclear biology or TE regulation.
