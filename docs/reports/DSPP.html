DSPP - Rejection Report

Rejection Summary

Gene: DSPP Protein: Dentin sialophosphoprotein UniProt: Q9NZW4

Reason for rejection: DSPP is a secreted extracellular matrix protein that is a critical component of dentin (tooth tissue). It is cleaved into dentin sialoprotein (DSP) and dentin phosphoprotein (DPP), both of which function in dentinogenesis and biomineralization. While DSPP has a nuclear GO-CC annotation (one of four: cytoplasm, extracellular matrix, extracellular region, nucleus), its primary and well-established localization is secreted extracellular matrix. UniProt lists its subcellular location as "Secreted, extracellular space, extracellular matrix." At 1301 aa, DSPP is a large protein, but it is proteolytically processed and functions entirely in the extracellular matrix to bind calcium and regulate hydroxyapatite crystal formation. The nuclear GO-CC annotation is likely an artifact. There is no evidence of nuclear function and no connection to TE regulation.
