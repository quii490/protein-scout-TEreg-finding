3. 详细分析

3.1 功能描述

Catalyzes the hydrolysis of an acyl chain from the tetra-acylated cardiolipin (CL) and produces the tri-acylated monolysocardiolipin (MLCL) (PubMed:40903572). Prefers CL with shorter and saturated fatty acyl chains as substrates (PubMed:40903572). Together with Tafazzin (TAZ), it participates in the mechanism of CL remodeling, a process consisting of repetitive cycles of hydrolysis and reacylation
