补充分析 (UniProt API)

蛋白全称: TBC1 domain family member 20

功能: GTPase-activating protein (GAP) specific for Rab1 and Rab2 small GTPase families for which it can accelerate the intrinsic GTP hydrolysis rate by more than five orders of magnitude (PubMed:23236136). Also shows GAP activity for RAB18 GTPase (PubMed:26063829). Promotes RAB18 dissociation from the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membrane into the cytosol, probably through stimulating RAB18 GTP-hydrolysis (PubMed:26063829). Involved in maintaining endoplasmic reticulum structure (PubMed:24891604)
