CEMIP2 - Rejection Report

Rejection Summary

Gene: CEMIP2 Protein: Cell surface hyaluronidase CEMIP2 UniProt: Q9UHN6

Reason for rejection: CEMIP2 is a cell surface hyaluronidase that mediates the initial cleavage of extracellular high-molecular-weight hyaluronan. Its GO-CC annotations are exclusively membrane/extracellular: extracellular exosome, membrane, plasma membrane. UniProt lists its sole subcellular location as "Cell membrane." There is no nuclear GO-CC term and no evidence of nuclear localization or function. At 1383 aa, it is a large protein, but its function is entirely extracellular and cell-surface associated, involved in hyaluronan catabolism, angiogenesis, and cell adhesion. There is no connection to nuclear biology or TE regulation.
