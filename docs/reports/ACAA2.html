ACAA2 - Rejection Report

Rejection Summary

Gene: ACAA2 Protein: 3-ketoacyl-CoA thiolase, mitochondrial UniProt: P42765

Reason for rejection: ACAA2 is a mitochondrial enzyme that catalyzes the final step of mitochondrial fatty acid beta-oxidation. It has no nuclear GO-CC annotations (only mitochondrial matrix and mitochondrion). UniProt lists its sole subcellular location as "Mitochondrion." There is no evidence of nuclear localization, nuclear function, or connection to TE regulation. This is a well-characterized metabolic enzyme with exclusively mitochondrial localization.
