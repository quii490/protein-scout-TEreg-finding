CRYGD - Rejection Report

Rejection Summary

Gene: CRYGD Protein: Gamma-crystallin D UniProt: P07320

Reason for rejection: CRYGD is a gamma-crystallin, one of the dominant structural components of the vertebrate eye lens. While it has both cytoplasm and nucleus GO-CC annotations, crystallins are lens-specific structural proteins. The nuclear GO-CC annotation is functionally irrelevant in this context - lens fiber cells degrade their nuclei during terminal differentiation, and crystallins fill the entire cell volume at extremely high concentrations. CRYGD has no enzymatic activity, no DNA/RNA binding capability, and no regulatory function relevant to TE biology. It is a tissue-specific structural protein with expression restricted to the eye lens. There is no plausible connection to TE regulation in any context.
