3. 详细分析

3.1 功能描述

Specific component of the MRP ribonucleoprotein endoribonuclease, Rnase/Mrp complex, a ribonucleoprotein complex involved in pre-rRNA processing (PubMed:28115465, PubMed:40413743). May play a role in cortex development as part of the Notch signaling pathway. Downstream of Notch may repress the expression of proneural genes and inhibit neuronal differentiation thereby maintaining neural progenitors
