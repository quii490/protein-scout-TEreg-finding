Replaces the canonical cytochrome c oxidase subunit COXFA4 in mitochondrial complex IV and targets it to degradation, attenuating mitochondrial respiration, reducing membrane potential as well as ROS generation and modulates innate immune signaling (PubMed:33837217, PubMed:34878835).
